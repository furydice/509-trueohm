Frequently asked questions

Is TrueOhm really free?

Yes. TrueOhm is completely free — no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, and no account.

Does TrueOhm work offline?

Yes. TrueOhm runs entirely on your device. Every calculation works without an internet connection.

What can it calculate?

The Ohm's Law wheel (volts, amps, ohms, watts), single- and three-phase AC power (kVA, kW, power factor), the power triangle (kW / kVAR / kVA), energy cost, and efficiency — each with the formula shown.

Is the output code-compliant?

TrueOhm uses standard electrical formulas. It is a field aid for qualified professionals, not a substitute for engineering judgment or the National Electrical Code — always verify a result before relying on it.
