Frequently asked questions

What can TrueOhm calculate?

Use the Ohm's Law wheel for volts, amps, resistance, and watts; calculate single- and three-phase AC power; work with the power triangle; estimate energy cost; and check efficiency. Results show the formula with your entered values.

How should I use a result?

TrueOhm is a calculation aid for qualified professionals. Verify results, equipment information, and application requirements before relying on an answer.
