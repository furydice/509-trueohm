TrueOhm is a fast, free Ohm's Law and power calculator for working electricians. Solve for any unknown, see the exact formula with your numbers plugged in, and get a warning when something doesn't add up.

Get support Privacy policy

/Ohm's Law wheel

Enter any two of volts, amps, ohms, or watts and TrueOhm solves the other two from the full 12-formula wheel.

/AC power, 1φ & 3φ

Relate volts, amps, kVA, kW, and power factor and solve the missing one. Three-phase applies the √3 to line-to-line voltage correctly.

/Power triangle

Enter any two of kW, kVAR, kVA, power factor, or angle and get the rest from S² = P² + Q².

/Energy cost & efficiency

kW × hours × $/kWh gives kWh used and dollar cost; efficiency is P out / P in as a percentage.

/Shows its work

Every result opens a how-this-was-calculated panel with the formula and your values substituted in — no black boxes.

/Private by design

Everything runs on your device — no account, no login, no internet required. We collect nothing. See our privacy policy.
