Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

TrueOhm ("the App") is developed and operated by 509 Electric LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), based in Yakima, Washington, United States. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

The short version: TrueOhm does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Every calculation you make stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. TrueOhm does not collect personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not require an account, name, email address, or login to use the App.
  • We do not track you, and the App contains no advertising or third‑party analytics SDKs.
  • The App does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • The App makes no network requests — it works fully offline.

How your data is stored

Any values or settings the App keeps are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard on‑device storage. This data is not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot access it. If you delete the App, this data is removed with it (subject to your device backups, which you control).

Data sharing

Because we do not collect your personal data, we have nothing to sell or share with third parties. We do not share, rent, or trade information about you with anyone.

Children's privacy

TrueOhm is a professional tool intended for electricians, apprentices, estimators, and inspectors. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, biometrics, and encryption). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and post the revised policy here. Material changes will be reflected in an App update where appropriate.
