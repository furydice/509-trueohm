Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

TrueOhm ("the App") is developed and operated by 509 Electric LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), based in Yakima, Washington, United States. This policy explains how the App handles information.

TrueOhm performs calculations on your device. Settings or values included in a device backup are handled by the backup services you choose.

App access

TrueOhm requires no account. The App is free to use, contains no ads or in-app purchases, and its calculations work offline.

Device settings and backups

The App may retain settings or values using the operating system's on-device storage. Your device and backup configuration determine whether that information is included in a backup and how the backup provider handles it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the date above and post the revised policy here.
